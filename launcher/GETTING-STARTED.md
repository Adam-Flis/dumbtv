# Your TV's home screen

This TV has a simpler home screen than the one Amazon ships: your apps, your HDMI inputs,
the time and the weather. No ads and no "recommended for you" rows.

Lost? **Press Home on the remote.** It always brings you back here.

## What's on the screen

- **Top left:** a greeting, the time and the date.
- **Top right:** the weather now and for the next few hours (if it's turned on).
- **Round buttons along the top**, from left to right:
  - **Inputs:** switch to a cable box, game console or anything else plugged into the TV.
  - **Wi-Fi:** shows whether the TV is online. Press it to open the Wi-Fi settings.
  - **Moon:** turns night mode on or off. Night mode dims the screen in the evening.
  - **Stopwatch:** the sleep timer. The TV turns itself off after the time you pick.
  - **Sliders:** Options, for changing how this screen looks.
  - **Gear:** Fire TV settings (network, apps, remotes and more).
- **Favorites:** the row of apps you use most.
- **Recently used:** the last few apps you opened.
- **All apps:** everything installed on the TV.

## The remote

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| ▲ ▼ ◀ ▶ | Move around the screen |
| OK (the middle button) | Open the app or button you're on |
| ☰ Menu, or hold OK | Move, rename or hide the app you're on |
| Back | Go back one step, or close a menu |
| Home | Come back to this home screen |

The TV also shows these in **Options › Help › Remote guide**.

## Everyday things

**Open an app:** select it and press OK.

**Change your favorites:** go to the end of the Favorites row and choose **Edit favorites**. Press OK on an app to add it or take it out.

**Move or hide an app:** select it, press ☰ Menu, then choose Move, Rename or Hide. To bring hidden apps back, open Options › More › Reset tiles.

**Change the order of the rows, or hide some:** Options › Home screen › Home screen layout. Press OK on a row to reorder it, ▲ ▼ to move it, OK to put it down. ◀ ▶ shows or hides a row.

**Sleep timer:** press the stopwatch button and pick a time, or choose **Custom…** for any length. While a timer is running, the same button shows the time left and lets you add time or cancel it.

**Night mode:** press the moon button to turn it on or off now. To set it to turn on and off by itself each evening, go to Options › Night.

**Screen saver:** if it's turned on, the TV shows photos with the time and weather after a few minutes without anyone pressing a button, on the home screen or in a paused app. Any button brings back what you were watching. After a while the TV goes to sleep by itself.

**Screen goes black after the screen saver:** that's Screen care, just before the TV sleeps. It protects some TVs from burn-in. Press any button and the screen comes back. You can turn it off in Options › Screen saver.

## If something seems wrong

1. **Press Home.** That fixes most things.
2. **Open Options › Help.** A green dot next to an item means it's working. An amber dot means it needs attention; press OK on it to see what to do.
3. **An app is stuck:** press Home, then open the app again.
4. **Everything is stuck:** restart the TV. Open the gear button, then Device & Software, then Restart. Or unplug the TV for 10 seconds.
5. **You need Amazon's own home screen** (to buy or rent something, for example): go to Options › More › Amazon home screen. Press Home to come back.

Still stuck? Call whoever set up the TV. The settings live on the TV, so nothing is lost if it restarts.
